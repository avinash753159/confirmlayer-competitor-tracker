Competitors

AgeChecker.Net

Age verification for Shopify and e-commerce stores with configurable age minimums and location based order blocking.

Pricing: $25/month plus $0.50 per accepted verification, plus enabled add-ons (from the Shopify App Store listing).

  • Website https://agechecker.net/
  • Pricing page not published
  • Shopify app https://apps.shopify.com/agechecker-net?surface_intra_position=1&surface_type=partners&surface_version=redesign
  • Demo booking https://agechecker.net/demo
  • Contact email contact@agechecker.net

BlueCheck

Age verification between payment and fulfillment using order details, with SSN and photo ID fallbacks.

Pricing: Small $25/month, Medium $100/month, Large $250/month, plus per-verification usage charges reported at about $0.50 per completed verification (from the Shopify App Store listing and third-party app review sources).

  • Website https://www.bluecheck.me
  • E-commerce page https://www.bluecheck.me/ecommerce-age-verification
  • Pricing page not published
  • Shopify app https://apps.shopify.com/bluecheck-age-verification?locale=nb&surface_detail=orders-and-shipping-orders-order-editing&surface_inter_position=1&surface_intra_position=8&surface_type=category&surface_version=redesign
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email merchant@bluecheck.me

AgeChecked

UK based post checkout age verification for Shopify and WooCommerce, closest to ConfirmLayer's wedge.

Pricing: Basic: free, then credit packs. Plus: $99.99/month. Credit packs: 100 checks $45 ($0.45/check), 250 checks $100 ($0.40/check), 500 checks $170 ($0.34/check), 1,000 checks $300 ($0.30/check). WooCommerce: starting at 40 GBP/month for 100 checks.

  • Website https://www.agechecked.com
  • Shopify page https://www.agechecked.com/shopify-age-verification/
  • E-commerce page https://www.agechecked.com/agechecked-for-ecommerce/
  • WooCommerce page https://www.agechecked.com/woocommerce-plugin/
  • Pricing page not published
  • Shopify app not published
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email not published

Veratad

Enterprise ID and age verification API with an Age Restricted Commerce vertical.

Pricing: None published; sales-led pricing.

  • Website https://veratad.com/
  • Age verification https://veratad.com/solutions
  • Pricing page not published
  • Shopify app not published
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email Info@veratad.com

Persona

Modular ID verification platform with government ID and selfie flows across 200+ countries.

Pricing: Free Starter plan: 500 government ID verifications per month. Essential plan: starting at $250/month (12-month minimum term). Startup Program: up to 12 months of free core-tool access.

  • Website https://withpersona.com/
  • Pricing page https://withpersona.com/pricing
  • Shopify app not published
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email startups@withpersona.com

Veriff

ID and biometric verification vendor with age validation for age restricted purchases.

Pricing: None published; sales-led pricing.

  • Website https://www.veriff.com/
  • KYC use cases https://www.veriff.com/use-cases/kyc
  • Pricing page not published
  • Shopify app not published
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email not published

ID Analyzer

Document and biometric IDV API with pay per verification and hosted verification links.

Pricing: Plans from $89/month. Free plan: $0/month with 100 free test credits. One credit per standard ID verification; all features (OCR, biometrics, liveness, fraud, AML) bundled in every plan. 25% off annual billing.

  • Website https://www.idanalyzer.com
  • Pricing page https://www.idanalyzer.com/en/pricing
  • Shopify app not published
  • Demo booking not published
  • Contact email not published

Pricing: Not public. Page states price varies by application/platform, means of integration, and number of monthly verifications.
