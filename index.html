Competitors

Eight vendors researched Sep 30, 2026. Four are direct wedge competitors for post-checkout e-commerce age gating.

AgeChecker.Net

Direct wedge

Age verification for e-commerce and Shopify stores. Verifies customers before or after checkout, supports location and product rules, integrates with Checkout, customer accounts and Shopify Flow.

$25/month plus $0.50 per accepted verification, plus enabled add-ons (Shopify App Store listing).
Links Website: https://agechecker.net/ Shopify app listing Demo page: https://agechecker.net/demo
Contact: contact@agechecker.net · Demo page phone 1-888-276-2303

BlueCheck

Direct wedge

Age verification between payment and fulfillment using order details already on hand (name and address, 96%+ match rate claimed). Fallbacks: last four of SSN, mobile-phone check, photo ID. Product and state rules, order tags, BigCommerce and REST API.

Small $25/month, Medium $100/month, Large $250/month, plus about $0.50 per completed verification (Shopify listing and app review sources).
Links Website: https://www.bluecheck.me E-commerce page Shopify app listing WordPress plugin page
Contact: merchant@bluecheck.me (published on the WordPress plugin page)

AgeChecked

Direct wedge

UK-based age verification for e-commerce. Shopify app and WooCommerce plugin for alcohol, knives and vaping. Post-checkout flow like ConfirmLayer's wedge: verify once per customer, merchant sees pass/fail and ships or follows up.

Basic: free, then credit packs. Plus: $99.99/month. Credits: 100 for $45 ($0.45/check), 250 for $100 ($0.40/check), 500 for $170 ($0.34/check), 1,000 for $300 ($0.30/check).
Links Website: https://www.agechecked.com Shopify product page E-commerce page WooCommerce plugin page
Contact: no published sales email found. Outreach via Monday.com form.

Verifymy (VerifyMyAge)

Direct wedge

Netherlands-based age verification for e-commerce. Dedicated Shopify app (zero friction, no impact on checkout), WooCommerce app, eBay-compatible app and direct API. Referenced by the Ofcom and UK age-check ecosystem.

Not public. Pricing page says price varies by application, platform, integration method and monthly volume.
Links Website: https://verifymyage.nl Pricing page: https://verifymyage.nl/pricing
Contact: no published sales email found. Outreach via verifymy.io/contact form.

Veratad

Partial fit

Identity and age verification via a single JSON REST API (IDresponse platform). ID documents, identity data for 5B+ citizens, knowledge-based authentication, biometrics, 2FA, reusable identity. Lists "Age-Restricted Commerce" as an industry.

None published. Sales-led pricing.
Links Website: https://veratad.com/ Age verification page: https://veratad.com/solutions
Contact: Info@veratad.com (site footer) · (201) 510-6000 · (888) 510-7343

Persona

Partial fit

Modular identity verification platform: government-ID and selfie flows, database verifications, fraud prevention, workflow orchestration, KYC/KYB templates. Covers 200+ countries. Age assurance is a supported verification type.

Free Starter: 500 government ID verifications/month. Essential: starting at $250/month (12-month minimum). Startup Program: up to 12 months of free core-tool access.
Links Website: https://withpersona.com/ Pricing page: https://withpersona.com/pricing
Contact: startups@withpersona.com (official startup-program page)

Veriff

Partial fit

ID and biometric verification with web/mobile SDK, APIs and webhooks. Government-ID plus selfie/liveness flows. KYC use-case page explicitly covers streamlining age-restricted purchases.

None published. Sales-led pricing.
Links Website: https://www.veriff.com/ KYC use cases: https://www.veriff.com/use-cases/kyc
Contact: no published sales email found. Outreach via contact-sales form.

ID Analyzer

Partial fit

Document and biometric identity verification API. OCR, biometric match, liveness, fraud signals, AML/PEP screening across 3,000+ document types from 190+ countries. DocuPass hosted verification links suit e-commerce age checks.

Plans from $89/month. Free plan: $0/month with 100 free test credits. One credit per standard ID verification. 25% off annual billing.
Links Website: https://www.idanalyzer.com Pricing page: https://www.idanalyzer.com/en/pricing
Contact: no published sales email found. Outreach via contact form.
